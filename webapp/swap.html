Swap a shift

Request a swap in under a minute.

Log the request here instead of the WhatsApp group. It lands in the rota sheet's SwapRequests tab, the supervisor is notified by email + Telegram, and you get a reference ID to track it.

Be specific — the supervisor approves against clinical cover, not vibes.

What happens after you submit

  1. 01Your request is validated and written to the SwapRequests tab of the rota sheet with a unique ID (SWP-…).
  2. 02The supervisor is notified instantly on email and Telegram with your shift, ward, role and reason.
  3. 03Once approved, the rota rows are updated (or reassigned to your partner) and both of you are told. Until then, your original shift stands.

Good to know

  • AA swap is only final when the rota shows it. A "yes" in a chat doesn't cover the ward.
  • BRequests closer to shift time are triaged first — at T-3h the supervisor is already being paged for unconfirmed shifts.
  • CRepeated last-minute swaps trigger a rota review with the charge nurse.
Urgent, under 2 hours? Don't rely on this form — phone the ward directly, then log the swap here so the record is complete.
