How ShiftHub works

One rota sheet. Six automated steps.

Everything runs in n8n — the same rota your facility already keeps, now with a brain and a heartbeat. Staff keep replying in chat; the system does the chasing.

1

Rota sheet

The rota lives in one Google Sheet — shifts, wards, roles, required headcount, assignment status.

2

T-1 day reminder

6pm the day before: each assigned staff member gets an email + Telegram reminder automatically.

3

T-2 hours reminder

A final nudge ~2 hours before the shift starts — the last chance to arrange a swap.

4

YES = confirmed

Staff reply YES (Telegram, email reply, or one-tap link) and the rota flips to CONFIRMED instantly.

5

3-hour escalation

Still unconfirmed at T-3h? The supervisor is alerted — before the ward opens, not after the no-show.

6

Summary & flags

Monthly hours per staff member, plus daily flags on shifts running below required headcount.

Inside the box

What ShiftHub ships with

Reminder engine

Two timed reminders per assignment (T-1 day 18:00, T-2 hours), delivered over both Telegram and email. Duplicate sends are stamped out via the rota itself.

YES confirmation

Three ways to confirm: reply YES to the Telegram bot, reply to the reminder email (watched over IMAP), or tap the one-time link in the email. Nearest upcoming shift is confirmed, rota updated, confirmation sent back.

3-hour supervisor alert

The heart of this project: every 15 minutes, any shift starting within ~3 hours with a pending assignment pages the supervisor — name, ward, role, minutes to start.

Swap requests

A public form (this site) posts to n8n; the request lands in the SwapRequests tab and the supervisor is notified by email + Telegram with full context and reason.

Understaffing flags

Daily 07:00 sweep of the next 14 days: any shift with fewer CONFIRMED staff than requiredCount gets flagged on the rows, logged to the Flags tab and sent to the supervisor.

Monthly hours summary

On the 1st of each month: confirmed hours per staff member (overnight shifts roll past midnight correctly), a supervisor report, optional per-staff copies, and a MonthlySummary tab.

Architecture

Boringly simple, deliberately.

No custom database, no server code to babysit — n8n orchestrates, Google Sheets is the database, and this static app is the front porch.
